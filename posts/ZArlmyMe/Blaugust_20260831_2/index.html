My Creator Appreciation

2026-08-31 | 我 | 阅读 | 624 字 | 3 分钟

On every year’s day 1, I make a list of the things I’ve really enjoyed last year, including books, comics, movies, television, documentaries, reality shows, albums, agricultural products, useful items, delicious food, software, websites… you can find it here: yearly best.

Because this article is too late for Blaugust, I’ve listed them all together, haha.

Many of the works in it are from Japan, Europe(I read mostly Poland, France, UK, Portugal), USA, thanks to China’s subtitling groups and publishers.

Favorite Films & TV Series:

  • 横道世之介/A Story of Yonosuke
  • 沙漏时镜下的疗养院/The Hourglass Sanatorium 1973 by Has
  • 陆上行舟/Fitzcarraldo
  • 在世界的尽头相遇/Encounters at the End of the World (documentary film)
  • 地球公民/Earthlings by Shaun Monson (documentary film)
  • 邮差/Il postino
  • 姑奶奶/Madame (documentary film)
  • 重启人生/Rebooting
  • 有熊谷守一在的地方/Mori, The Artist’s Habitat
  • 火花/Hibana: Spark
  • 更美好的事/Better Things
  • 真探S1/True Detective Season 1
  • 火线S2/The Wire Season 2
  • 听风的歌/Heima by Sigur Rós (documentary film)
  • 风骚律师/Better Call Saul
  • 马男波杰克/BoJack Horseman

Favorite Books:

  • 荒原狼/Steppenwolf by Hermann Hesse
  • 复杂/Complexity: A Guided Tour by Melanie Mitchell
  • 鹿之民/People of the Deer by Farley Mowat
  • 火花/Spark by Naoki Matayoshi
  • 鳄鱼街/The Street of Crocodiles by Bruno Schulz
  • 赫尔佐格论赫尔佐格/Werner Herzog A Guide for the Perplexed: Conversations with Paul Cronin
  • 活了100万次的猫/The Cat That Lived A Million Times by Yoko Sano
  • 坛经/The Altar-Sutra by Huineng
  • 白鲸/Moby Dick by Herman Melville
  • 四次讲座/Quatre Conférences by Claude Simon

Favorite Animation & Comics:

  • Hunter × Hunter
  • 阿兰的战争/Alan’s War by Emmanuel Guibert
  • 万物/Alpha… directions by Jens Harder
  • A Brown World/The Nao of Brown by Glyn Dillon
  • 葬送的芙莉莲/Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End by Kanehito Yamada
  • 乒乓/Ping Pong by Taiyo Matsumoto
  • 蘑菇的拟态日常/Shimeji Simulation
  • 80亿个精灵/Eight Billion Genies by Charles Soule
  • 凉宫春日的忧郁/The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya by Nagaru Tanigawa

Favorite Musicians:

声音玩具（Sound Toy）, 秘密后院（Secretby）, 超载（Overload）, Muse, Rhapsody, Galaxie 500, Green Day, CAN, Carsick Cars, The White Stripes, Equilibrium, Sigur Rós, Super Furry Animals, PAIN, Mayumi Kojima, Blind Guardian, Björk

My Idols/Heros:

Laozi & Zhuangzi, Huineng, Werner Herzog, 邱志杰/Qiu Zhijie, 木心/Mu Xin, Ted Kaczynski("for his critique thoughts of industrial civilization), Simone Weil, Michel Foucault, Agnès Varda, Kiki Kirin, Nassim Taleb, Douglas Hofstadter

I’m actually a bit like a hunter, one loves to go out and search. When I enter a new field, I dig around and find out what really hits me. I wonder what you’ll think after reading the lists above. Interesting? Complex? Or maybe a little curious about me, haha.

Over time, I found myself becoming quite difficult to move, since I had been so deeply moved before, the new can rarely reach that scale. I am not a game guy, I only tried playing Black Myth: Wukong, but my skills were too poor, so I gave up. However, I still finished the entire story and was deeply moved. Games are another huge world
